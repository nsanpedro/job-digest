Elena Marchetti
Senior Graphic Designer based in Barcelona

Seven years designing brand systems, editorial layouts, and packaging for consumer and cultural clients. I lead visual identity work end to end: discovery, moodboards, logo exploration, type pairings, color systems, and the full guideline document that ships to the client. Comfortable presenting three concept routes to a founder and defending typographic choices in front of a marketing director.

Most recently Senior Designer at Estudio Paloma (Barcelona, 2023 to present), where I rebuilt the brand for a mid-sized natural wine importer: wordmark, secondary marks, a custom display cut based on a 1930s Spanish specimen, packaging for 14 SKUs, and a 60-page brand book. Before that, in-house Brand Designer at Novena Editorial (Madrid, 2020 to 2023), designing book covers, interior layouts in InDesign, and the seasonal catalog. Started as a junior at Studio Falso (Milan, 2018 to 2020), where I did a lot of poster work for a chamber music festival and learned to hand-letter properly.

Daily tools: Figma for UI-adjacent brand work and component libraries, Adobe Illustrator and InDesign for print and identity, Photoshop for retouching, Glyphs 3 for type customization, After Effects when a mark needs to move. I run my own print production checks — bleed, overprint, spot colors, paper stock conversations with the printer. Comfortable specifying Pantone, CMYK builds, and foil finishes.

I care about typography as craft: rag control, kerning tables, optical sizes, and the difference between a workhorse serif and a display face pretending to be one. I sketch a lot on paper before opening any file. My work has been picked up by It's Nice That and Brand New; I don't chase awards but I do read the critique.

Interested in senior graphic designer, brand designer, or visual identity lead roles at a studio or an in-house brand team.
